# MediBook – Creative Direction (Stage A & B)

**Brand:** MediBook  
**Positioning:** Editorial, human-centered, architectural healthcare discovery and appointment management.

## 1. Visual & Editorial Principles
- **Identity:** Text-only wordmark `MediBook` rendered in editorial serif/sans pairing with zero decorative logo icons, medical crosses, or third-party brand marks.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Canvas: Warm Ivory (`#FAF7F2`)
  - Elevated Surface: Soft Off-White (`#FFFFFF`) & Muted Beige (`#F3EFE6`)
  - Deep Editorial Band: Deep Charcoal (`#1C1917`)
  - Primary Ink: Rich Charcoal (`#1C1917`) & Warm Grey (`#57534E`)
  - Accent: Deep Forest Green (`#1B4332`, hover `#143326`)
  - Hairline Borders: Warm Stone (`#E6E0D4`)
- **Typography:**
  - Display Headlines: `Cormorant Garamond` (editorial serif, tight tracking `-0.02em`, expressive scale)
  - UI, Navigation & Body: `Inter` (clean modern sans-serif, high legibility)
- **Anti-Slop Guardrails:** No purple/blue gradients, no floating decorative blobs, no fake statistics or testimonials, no generic SaaS card grids.
