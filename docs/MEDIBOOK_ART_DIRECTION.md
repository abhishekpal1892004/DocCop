# DocCop — Art Direction (Stage 1)

**Brand:** DocCop  
**Positioning:** Luxury editorial healthcare discovery and clinical care coordination.

## 1. Audit of Previous Design Weaknesses
- Repetitive warm-beige background across every section.
- Generic 2-column hero with a rectangular photo beside centered-feel text.
- Plain horizontal 4-input search rectangle.
- Generic dark rectangle closing CTA.
- Inconsistent brand naming (`MediBook` vs `DocCop`).

## 2. New Visual Language
- **Wordmark:** Text-only `DocCop` (zero decorative logos, crosses, or icons).
- **Surface Contrast:** Alternating warm porcelain (`#F7F5F0`), soft white (`#FFFFFF`), deep ink (`#171916`), and full-bleed natural-light photography.
- **Typography Trio:**
  - Display Serif: `Cormorant Garamond` (asymmetric line breaks, editorial scale).
  - UI & Body Sans: `Inter` (crisp legibility).
  - Monospace Metadata: `JetBrains Mono` (small uppercase section indices & clinical metadata).
- **Accent Discipline:** Deep forest (`#183F32`) for primary actions, restrained copper (`#A87957`) for editorial indices, muted olive (`#777C68`) and stone (`#D9D5CA`) for structure.
